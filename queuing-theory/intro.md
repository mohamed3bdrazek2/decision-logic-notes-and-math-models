# Introduction to Queuing Theory

Queuing Theory is the mathematical study of waiting lines, or queues. It provides models to predict queue lengths and waiting times, making it essential for optimizing traffic flow and system capacity in large-scale networks.

## Little's Law

A fundamental theorem in queuing theory is Little's Law. It states that the long-term average number of items in a stable system is equal to the long-term average arrival rate multiplied by the average time an item spends in the system.

$$L = \lambda W$$

Where:
* $L$ is the average number of items in the queuing system.
* $\lambda$ (lambda) is the average arrival rate of items.
* $W$ is the average time an item spends in the system.
